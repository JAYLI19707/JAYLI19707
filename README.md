## About Me


My interests lie at the intersection of **large language models, recommender systems, and machine learning systems**. I enjoy understanding how models work, reproducing research ideas, and building reliable systems that connect algorithms with real-world applications.

- **Research interests:** LLMs, generative recommendation (LLM4Rec), reasoning, retrieval and ranking, efficient machine learning.
- **Engineering interests:** model implementation, data pipelines, system reliability, and edge/embedded computing.
- **Current focus:** graph-guided reasoning for generative recommendation and the foundations of modern deep learning.

## Research & Engineering

- **LLM-based Generative Recommendation** — Explored reasoning-enhanced generative recommendation with Qwen3-1.7B and semantic item identifiers; studied the relationship between recommendation quality, reasoning behavior, and computational efficiency.
- **Transformer from Scratch** — Implemented and pretrained a decoder-only Transformer, working with components including RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, grouped-query attention, and causal attention; also explored supervised fine-tuning and preference optimization.
- **Embedded Intelligent Monitoring** — Worked on a Loongson 2K300 and OpenHarmony-based monitoring and data acquisition system, spanning device integration, Qt application development, local data storage, and communication.
